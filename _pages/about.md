I am an Associate Professor at the University of Udine, working with the AVIRES/MLP Labs on machine learning and computer vision. My research spans visual tracking and person and vehicle re-identification, as well as image colorization, food recognition, medical imaging, and super-resolution.

I lead and contribute to national and international research projects, including work on agricultural monitoring and autonomous agents. I teach computer science at bachelor, master, and doctoral levels, and contribute to the research community through conference organization, editorial service, and doctoral supervision.
